<div class="home-intro">
<p>I am Zheng Zhang, a Senior Algorithm Researcher at HiSilicon Linx Lab, Huawei. I received my master's degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) and joined Huawei in 2022. From 2022 to 2025, I worked at the Huawei Cloud Media Innovation Lab on 3D vision and 3D generation; since 2025, I have been with HiSilicon Linx Lab.</p>

<p>My research spans 3D generation, 3D vision, and Embodied Intelligence. Recently, I have been focusing on World Action Models for Embodied Intelligence.</p>
